# 🤖 Artificial Intelligence
## What Artificial Intelligence Is

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is software designed to learn, reason, and assist with complex tasks. It enables machines to analyze information, identify patterns, and make decisions that previously required human judgment.

AI forms the foundation of the **Intelligence Layer** within the Satoshium architecture.

---

# 🔎 The Simple Definition

Artificial Intelligence is software that can learn from data and use that learning to make decisions or generate outcomes.

AI does not think like humans.  
It analyzes patterns and predicts outcomes based on large amounts of information.

---

# ⚙️ What AI Can Do

Artificial intelligence systems can:

- analyze information by detecting patterns in datasets
- generate content such as text, images, software, and ideas
- assist decision-making by evaluating possible outcomes
- automate tasks repeatedly and efficiently

These capabilities enable the emergence of autonomous software systems.

---

# 🧠 How AI Works (Simplified)

Modern AI systems operate through several stages:

**Training**

Models learn statistical patterns from large datasets.

**Inference**

The model applies what it learned to new inputs.

**Feedback**

Outputs improve through correction and evaluation.

**Iteration**

Systems evolve through repeated refinement cycles.
