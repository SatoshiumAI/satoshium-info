🟠 Bitcoin Perspective First

Savings: Start with Bitcoin

Begin with Bitcoin as a savings technology, then compare it against the familiar fiat system most people use every day.

Lesson 01 of the Bitcoin vs Fiat financial education path.

🟠 Bitcoin Perspective

Savings

Bitcoin treats savings as something that should be hard to dilute over time.

Its fixed supply, predictable issuance, and open verification make Bitcoin different from money systems that can expand when institutions decide conditions require it.

Why this matters: This creates a long-horizon mindset: preserve value first, then decide how and when to spend it.
🟢 Fiat Counterpoint

Savings

Fiat money prioritizes flexibility, broad acceptance, and economic management.

Fiat works because people, businesses, banks, and governments accept it for everyday transactions. Its flexibility can help systems respond to crises, but that flexibility can also affect long-term purchasing power.

Why this matters: The comparison is not about dismissing fiat. It is about understanding the tradeoff between managed flexibility and fixed scarcity.
