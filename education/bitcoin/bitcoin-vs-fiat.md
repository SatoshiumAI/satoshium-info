# 🟠 Bitcoin vs Fiat

Fiat is government-issued currency.

Bitcoin is decentralized digital money with a fixed supply.

Understanding the difference explains why Bitcoin exists.

---

## 📌 One-Sentence Summary

Fiat is managed by institutions.

Bitcoin is governed by rules enforced by nodes.

This difference affects supply, trust, settlement, and ownership.

---

## ⚖️ Supply Differences

Fiat supply can expand through:

monetary policy  
credit creation  
central bank intervention  

Bitcoin supply is fixed:

maximum 21,000,000 BTC

Issuance follows a predictable schedule enforced by the protocol.

No authority can change this without global consensus.

---

## 🏛️ Control Models

Fiat systems operate through:

central banks  
commercial banks  
regulatory frameworks  

Bitcoin operates through:

distributed nodes  
miners  
open-source consensus rules  

No central operator exists.

Participation is voluntary and verifiable.

---

## 🔍 Trust Models

Fiat requires:

trust in issuers  
trust in institutions  
trust in policy stability  

Bitcoin allows:

verification of supply  
verification of transactions  
verification of rules  

Bitcoin replaces institutional trust with protocol transparency.

---

## 🧾 Settlement Differences

Fiat settlement depends on:

banking rails  
clearing systems  
intermediaries  

Transactions may be:

reversible  
frozen  
restricted  

Bitcoin settlement:

occurs on-chain  
becomes final after confirmations  
does not require permission from intermediaries  

Finality is enforced by the network itself.

---

## 🌍 Portability

Fiat transfers across borders depend on:

banks  
payment processors  
jurisdictional approval  

Bitcoin transfers require:

keys  
network access  

Value can move globally without relying on financial intermediaries.

---

## 🔐 Ownership Differences

Fiat balances represent:

claims on institutions  

Bitcoin can represent:

direct self-custody ownership  

Self-custody means controlling the keys.

Control of keys equals control of funds.

---

## 📉 Inflation Risk

Fiat purchasing power may change over time due to:

policy decisions  
currency expansion  
economic conditions  

Bitcoin issuance is predictable and capped.

Price volatility exists.

Supply uncertainty does not.

---

## ⚠️ Failure Modes

Fiat systems can fail through:

bank insolvency  
capital controls  
policy shocks  
currency debasement  

Bitcoin risks include:

key loss  
custody mistakes  
scams  

Bitcoin shifts risk from institutions to users.

Responsibility increases with sovereignty.

---

## 🧠 What Fiat Does Well

Fiat supports:

local commerce  
credit markets  
consumer protections  
price stability in the short term  

It is optimized for transactional economies.

---

## 🧱 What Bitcoin Does Differently

Bitcoin enables:

fixed supply savings  
permissionless participation  
independent verification  
global settlement  

It is optimized for long-term resilience and sovereign ownership.

---

## 📊 Why This Matters

Comparing fiat and Bitcoin clarifies tradeoffs:

Fiat prioritizes convenience

Bitcoin prioritizes certainty

Fiat prioritizes reversibility

Bitcoin prioritizes finality

Fiat prioritizes institutional trust

Bitcoin prioritizes verification

Both systems serve different roles.
