# 🟠 What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is digital money governed by rules instead of institutions.

It allows people anywhere in the world to send value directly without needing banks, payment processors, or central authorities.

Bitcoin is open, verifiable, and enforced by consensus.

---

## 📌 Simple Definition

Bitcoin is a global peer-to-peer monetary network secured by cryptography and proof-of-work.

It enables ownership, transfer, and verification of value without relying on trust in intermediaries.

---

## ✅ What Bitcoin Gives Humanity

Bitcoin introduced several capabilities that previously did not exist together in one system:

digital scarcity  
self-custody ownership  
global permissionless access  
verifiable monetary rules  
durable decentralized infrastructure  

Anyone can inspect how it works.

Anyone can participate.

No authority controls its issuance.

---

## 🔍 Why Bitcoin Exists

Traditional monetary systems depend on institutional trust.

Users must rely on:

banks  
governments  
policy makers  
financial intermediaries  

Bitcoin introduced a different model:

a public ledger anyone can verify  
predictable supply rules  
consensus enforcement without permission  

Trust moved from institutions to mathematics.

---

## ⚙️ How Bitcoin Works (Plain English)

Bitcoin operates through several interacting components:

the blockchain  
cryptographic keys  
mining  
nodes  

The blockchain records transactions in linked blocks.

Private keys authorize spending.

Mining secures the ledger using proof-of-work.

Nodes verify the rules independently.

Together, these create a system that resists manipulation.

---
