# 🔑 Seed Phrase Safety

Your seed phrase is the master key to your bitcoin.

If someone gets it, they control your funds.

If you lose it, you may lose access permanently.

Seed phrase protection is the foundation of self-custody.

---

## 📌 What a Seed Phrase Is

A seed phrase (also called a recovery phrase or mnemonic) is a sequence of words that recreates the private keys to your wallet.

This means:

- whoever has the seed phrase controls the bitcoin
- the wallet device does not hold your funds
- the seed phrase restores your wallet anywhere

Think of the wallet as a viewer.

The seed phrase is the vault key.

---

## ⚠️ How People Lose Bitcoin

Most losses are preventable.

Common causes include:

- taking photos of seed phrases
- saving them in notes apps
- emailing copies to yourself
- entering them into fake websites
- responding to “support” messages
- keeping only one backup
- assuming memory is enough

Bitcoin losses are rarely technical failures.

They are ordinary mistakes.

---

## 🛡️ The Golden Rules

Follow these rules permanently:

Never type your seed phrase into a website

Never store it digitally

Never share it with anyone

Always keep at least two backups

Test recovery once before trusting your setup

Your goal is protection from:

theft  
accidents  
forgetfulness  

---

## 📝 A Safe Backup Setup

A simple structure works for most users.

Step 1 — Write the phrase offline

Write clearly and in order

Check spelling carefully

Never read the phrase aloud near microphones or smart devices

Step 2 — Create a second backup

Two backups protect against:

fire  
water  
loss  
moving homes  

Store backups separately.

Never side by side.

Step 3 — Label discreetly

Do not label the backup “Bitcoin seed”

Use neutral language only you understand

Redundancy plus discretion creates resilience.

---

## 📄 Paper vs Metal Backups

Paper backups are:

easy  
cheap  
fast  

but vulnerable to:

fire  
water  
aging  

Metal backups are:

durable  
long-term  
environment resistant  

If bitcoin is a long-term asset, use long-term storage media.

---

## 🔍 Recovery Testing

A backup is not real until tested.

Safe recovery testing means:

using the wallet’s official recovery process

verifying the expected wallet appears

performing the test calmly, not during an emergency

Recovery testing builds confidence before problems occur.

---

## 🚫 Never-Do List

Never:

enter your seed phrase into websites

store it in cloud storage

save it in email drafts

photograph it

share it with support agents

keep only one copy

If someone asks for your seed phrase, the interaction is malicious.

No exceptions exist.

---

## 👪 Inheritance Considerations

Seed phrases create responsibility across time.

A safe setup should include:

clear instructions for trusted family members

separate storage from access instructions

documentation that explains recovery steps without exposing secrets

Security includes survivability.

---

## ❓ Quick FAQ

Is a seed phrase the same as a password?

No.

Passwords protect accounts.

Seed phrases recreate wallets.

Seed phrases are more powerful.

Should beginners split their seed phrase?

Usually no.

Splitting increases complexity and recovery risk.

Start with two complete backups in separate locations.

Should family members know where it is?

They should know how recovery works.

They should not casually access the phrase itself.

Process knowledge is safer than secret sharing.
