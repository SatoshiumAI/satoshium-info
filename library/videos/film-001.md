# 🎞️ Film 001 — The Quiet Future Is Being Built

A cinematic introduction to the Satoshium vision.

AI governed by Bitcoin. Built quietly. In public.

---

## 📌 Overview

Film 001 introduces the foundational idea behind Satoshium:

systems governed by verifiable rules instead of institutional discretion.

It presents Bitcoin as the base layer of truth for value and positions Satoshium as an extension of that model into intelligence.

---

## 🧭 Themes Introduced

This film establishes several long-horizon principles:

AI should operate under rules, not authority  
verification should replace trust  
infrastructure should be inspectable  
progress should be public  

These ideas define the architectural direction of the Satoshium platform.
