# 🌐 Satoshium Info
## The Knowledge Layer of the Satoshium Ecosystem

Satoshium Info is the documentation, architecture, and development intelligence surface of the Satoshium ecosystem.

It exists to make the project understandable, navigable, and durable — not just for today’s build cycle, but for the long arc of what Satoshium could become by 2035 and beyond.

If **satoshium.ai** is the public-facing platform, then **satoshium.info** preserves structure, maps systems, organizes work, and keeps the deeper logic of the ecosystem visible over time.

---

## 📚 Knowledge Layer (Phase004 Active) Role

The Satoshium Knowledge Layer establishes the shared vocabulary and conceptual reference system used across the platform.

It supports:

- canonical terminology registry
- architecture and system vocabulary
- governance and trust model definitions
- simulation and foresight terminology
- infrastructure and protocol concepts

Shared vocabulary enables consistent architecture, clear communication, and long-term interoperability across the Satoshium ecosystem.

---

## Why Satoshium Info Exists

Complex systems fail when their structure becomes invisible.

Satoshium Info exists to prevent that.

It provides a place to:

- preserve architecture
- document growth
- maintain continuity across domains
- connect experiments to systems
- explain long-horizon design decisions

This domain is not just a workspace.

It is the emerging **memory layer of Satoshium**.

## The Satoshium Domain Architecture

Satoshium Info is one layer within a broader multi-domain ecosystem.

Each domain has a distinct role:

| Domain | Role |
|-------|------|
| satoshium.ai | Public platform and primary introduction |
| satoshium.info | Knowledge layer and documentation hub |
| satoshium.xyz | Experimental systems and agent testing |
| satoshium.net | Infrastructure and network topology |
| satoshium.store | Cultural and economic layer |
| satoshium.link | Routing and coordination layer |
| satoshium.dev | Builder and contributor surface |

Together these domains form a layered architecture rather than a single flat website.
